# Work Report Demo — storyboard v1

## Decisions

- **Message:** I turned five businesses' manual spreadsheet work into automated, auditable systems with live dashboards — and cut ~$33K a year in recurring costs.
- **Audience & arc:** portfolio viewers; outcome hook → pattern → proof ×5 → savings → infrastructure → principles.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, ~4:30. Voiceover yes (Gemini Kore, first person). Captions yes, burned in, word-synced, in a bottom band (y 930–1040) — footage and graphics live in the top ~85%. Music bed yes, quiet, carved under the voice.
- **Spine:** the **pipeline rail** — five stations `SOURCE → VERIFY → SHEET → DASHBOARD → HUMAN` introduced in Frame 2, then docked as a slim rail at the top of every business scene (3–8), lighting the station the footage is showing (sheet footage lights SHEET, dashboard footage lights DASHBOARD, verifier/updater cards light VERIFY). It returns full-size in Frame 11 as the sign-off.
- **Brand (captured from the dashboards in the recording):**
  - Forest `#143221` (canvas, from the dashboard headers) · Cream `#FFFFF4` (foreground/paper, from dashboard bodies) · Gold `#E2B04A` (single accent — focal numbers, active rail station) · Mint `#93DBB3` (captured header mono text — "verified/ok" only) · Brick `#C2412D` (semantic only — conflicts, NEEDS RULING, "was" prices).
  - Type: **Newsreader** (display serif, echoes the dashboards' serif headings; embedded via local @font-face) · **IBM Plex Mono** (labels, rail, metadata — echoes the dashboards' letter-spaced mono) · **Montserrat** 700 (captions only, for legibility).
  - Radii 14px on cards, 18px on the browser frame. Easing: `power3.out` entrances, `expo.inOut` camera moves, `sine.inOut` ambient.
- **Footage treatment:** recording placed in a browser-window frame (1600×788 at 16:9-safe position), scaled, sped 1.5–2× through scrolls, with punch-ins on safe UI. **Privacy:** data regions are frosted (strong blur) per segment; crisp keep-zones only on headers, tab bars, KPI *labels*, buttons and status pills. Never visible: Gandi's name/email, tenant/person names, agentmail addresses, exact amounts in footage, Sawera's Google account email.
- **Bans:** no fake product UI (footage is the real product; cards only *explain*), no glow/neon, no gradient text, no centered card-grid slideshows, no stock icons for businesses, no stacking Afrofarms savings on top of $33K, no "three countries". Motion failures to avoid: the **slideshow** (every line a fresh card) and the **screensaver** (rail pulsing for no reason — the rail only moves when the station changes).
- **Held frame:** Frame 9 — the "$33,081 / year" figure lands and holds ~1.5s with no voice before the Manus line.
- **Truthfulness:** all dashboards and sheets are the real recording. All numbers come from the Notion Final Report. Motion-graphic scenes (2, 9, 10, 11) are explanatory, not product UI.
